# WordNet Attribution

This app may include compact offline dictionary data generated from Open English WordNet 2025.

Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet by the Open English WordNet Community and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.

- Open English WordNet downloads: https://en-word.net/downloads
- Open English WordNet GitHub: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet
- Princeton WordNet: https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
- CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Cambridge Dictionary is used only as an external online lookup link. Cambridge definitions, examples, and translations are not downloaded, scraped, bundled, or cached as offline dictionary data.
